glyphsketch

Draw a character. Everything runs in this page; nothing is sent anywhere.

Some characters look the same when drawn, such as Latin A, Greek Α and Cyrillic А. Each tile stands for one such group and shows the member your keyboard's language uses: A with English, Α with Greek. The others are in the tile's menu. The setting doesn't change what is recognized, only which look-alike a tile shows.

Tap a tile to type it; long-press or right-click for its look-alikes.
